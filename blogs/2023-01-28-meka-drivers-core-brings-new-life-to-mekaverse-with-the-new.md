Manticore Games and MekaVerse are excited to unveil Meka-Drivers, a first of its kind NFT PFP collection with unique built-in utility as they are instantly playable inside the Core Metaverse. This innovative collaboration demonstrates how NFTs can bring real, connected, and tangible experiences to owners. Meka-Driver owners will be able to see their unique NFT transformed and fully realized as beautifully rendered, 3D characters playable in Core.
