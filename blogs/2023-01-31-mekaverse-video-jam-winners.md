The $50,000 MekaVerse Jam is a collaboration between MekaVerse and Core that challenged the community to create MekaVerse art, games, stories, and videos in a variety of jams from Nov 29, 2022 to Jan 2, 2023. For the Video Jam, we challenged participants to produce a video that shows the beauty of MekaVerse using the creations on Core. The top 3 entries won $1,000, $600, and $400.
