As farmers harvest crops and people snuggle up next to a fire, Core creators were busy making amazing projects to celebrate the season. The Harvest Jam was a one-week contest that gave $100 rewards to the top two contestants in 3 categories: Decor, Scenes, and Interactables . There was also a bonus prize of $200 for the most retweeted submission.
