The $50,000 MekaVerse Jam is a collaboration between MekaVerse and Core that challenged the community to create MekaVerse art, games, stories, and videos in a variety of jams from Nov 29, 2022 to Jan 2, 2023. For the Game Jam, we challenged participants to create an experience that puts Mekas at the front of the action using Core. The top 3 entries won $10,000, $5,000, or $2,500.
