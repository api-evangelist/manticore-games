The $50,000 MekaVerse Jam is a collaboration between MekaVerse and Core that challenged the community to create MekaVerse art, games, stories, and videos in a variety of jams from Nov 29, 2022 to Jan 2, 2023. For the Art Jam, we challenged participants to create an environment from the MekaVerse World Map using Core. There were 3 categories available: Sci-Fi Cities , Forests , and Extreme Environments .
