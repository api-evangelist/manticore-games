Spookiness is in the air this time of year, and Core was no exception. Spooky Jam was a one-week contest that gave $100 rewards to the top two contestants in 3 different categories: Spooky Activities and Interactables, Dark Atmosphere and Décor, and Haunts and Homes. All these contestants have published their work to Community Content or Community Projects, so feel free to find them for your projects.
